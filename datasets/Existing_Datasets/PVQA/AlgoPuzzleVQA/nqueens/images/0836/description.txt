You are given a 10 * 10 chessboard. The objective is to place 10 chess queens on this board so that no two queens threaten each other; i.e. no two queens share the same row, column, or diagonal. 8 queens have already been placed in some of the squares of the board. Suppose you pick two squares to place the two remaining queen pieces in a way that fulfills the objective. Determine the position of the squares to place the remaining two queens.
